Putting the data in context

People are more
than a percentage.

Statistics show the scale of the need. They do not define an individual’s experience, strengths, or potential.

01One person may experience more than one condition.

Anxiety, depression, trauma-related conditions, and substance use disorders can overlap. Adding these figures would count some people more than once.

02The population and the time period matter.

“Past year” differs from “current diagnosis.” Some studies cover all adults; others cover ages 18–65, children, or people ages 12 and older.

03Older estimates are labeled clearly.

Several widely cited figures use older national surveys. We retain the original study dates and explain the methods behind each estimate.
