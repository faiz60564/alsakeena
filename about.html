About AlSakeena

Rooted in compassion and guided by purpose, we work to help people facing mental illnesses find the support, dignity, and opportunities they deserve.

Our Vision

A world where people living with mental illnesses have the care, support, and opportunities to live with dignity, belonging, and hope.

Our Mission

AlSakeena supports people living with mental illnesses through coordinated legal advocacy, access to treatment, community connection, and pathways to meaningful work and economic stability.

Our Values

Dignity

We respect every person’s worth, choices, and potential.

Compassion

We listen without judgment and respond with empathy.

Whole-person support

We recognize that mental health, relationships, legal rights, and financial stability are connected.

Equity and access

We work to reduce barriers to care, support, and opportunity.

Self-determination

We help people pursue recovery and independence on their own terms.

Accountability

We communicate honestly, use resources responsibly, and learn from the people we serve.
