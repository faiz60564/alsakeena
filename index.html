Disproportionate Impact of TRD

Individuals with Treatment-Resistant Depression incur 3.5x higher annual direct healthcare costs ($19,600+ vs $5,600) and experience 2.5x more disability days than non-TRD patients.

Human & Economic Burden

The Cascading Vicious Spiral

Without integrated support, a mental health crisis rapidly devolves into financial ruin and legal disenfranchisement.

1

Job Loss & Unemployment

Severe exhaustion, cognitive fog, and inability to maintain daily performance lead to job loss.

2

Loss of Healthcare

Employment termination immediately revokes health insurance, cutting off access to ongoing care.

3

Functional Inability

Inability to maintain household responsibilities, care for family, or manage basic daily living.

4

Complex Legal Maze

Navigating bureaucratic government disability systems results in high denial rates without legal aid.

AlSakeena Intervenes At Every Stage

Connecting clinical recovery directly with legal advocacy and financial safety nets.

Clinical Framework

Understanding Anxiety, MDD & Treatment Resistance

Severe mental health conditions are complex neurobiological illnesses — not personal failures or temporary emotional stress.

Clinical Anxiety Disorders

Persistent nervous system hyper-arousal impairing decision-making and cognitive resilience.

Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

Severe mood dysfunction causing emotional paralysis, anhedonia, and severe executive slowing.

What is TRD?

High-Risk Category

Standard Care Failure

Diagnosed when a patient fails to achieve remission after two or more adequate trials of conventional antidepressants.

570+ Day Episodes

Average TRD episodes persist for over 1.5 years (~3x longer than standard depressive episodes).

Insurance Gap

Standard insurance frequently excludes coverage for effective interventional treatments (Ketamine, TMS).

Functional Paralysis

Leads directly to loss of gainful employment, self-isolation, and severe socio-economic collapse.
