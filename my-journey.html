The Struggle

Alongside my career, I faced years of depression and anxiety. My symptoms became severe in 2021. Despite trying more than 20 medications, I struggled to find relief. It was an exhausting and isolating time.

A Turning Point

With the support of a compassionate care team, I found a different treatment approach. Over a series of treatments, I began to experience meaningful improvement and, for the first time in a long while, renewed hope.

Recovery and Renewal

My outlook, energy, and sense of possibility improved significantly. I began reconnecting with the people and activities I love, and looking toward the future with purpose.

A Bigger Purpose

That experience inspired me to establish AlSakeena. I want people facing mental illnesses to know they are not alone and to have access to compassionate support, care, community, and opportunities to rebuild their lives.

Bringing It All Together

AlSakeena brings together my professional leadership experience and personal journey of recovery. It is a commitment to turning empathy into action—helping others find stability, purpose, opportunity, and economic dignity.
