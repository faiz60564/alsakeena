Dr. Akhtar K. Siddiqui, MD

Medical Advisor — Pulmonary & Critical Care Medicine

Dr. Akhtar K. Siddiqui is a distinguished physician with more than four decades of experience in internal medicine, pulmonary disease, and critical care medicine. Board-certified in all three specialties, he brings extensive clinical knowledge and experience caring for individuals with complex medical conditions.

Dr. Siddiqui earned his medical degree from Osmania Medical College in Hyderabad, India, and completed postgraduate medical training in Chicago, followed by fellowship training in pulmonary medicine at the University of Missouri Medical Center.

Affiliated with Rush Copley Medical Center in Aurora, Illinois, Dr. Siddiqui has devoted his career to the diagnosis and treatment of respiratory illnesses, management of critically ill patients, and delivery of comprehensive medical care.

As a Medical Advisor to AlSakeena, Dr. Siddiqui brings a valuable perspective on the relationship between physical health, chronic illness, and overall well-being. His medical expertise will help inform the organization's commitment to compassionate, coordinated support for individuals navigating long-term mental health challenges.

Through his advisory involvement, he supports AlSakeena's mission of Restoring Peace & Economic Dignity.

Catherine Sinclair, PMHNP

Clinical Advisor — Mental Health

Catherine Sinclair is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with clinical experience across inpatient psychiatric care, partial hospitalization and intensive outpatient programs, and outpatient mental health services. Her professional background encompasses the assessment and treatment of individuals experiencing a broad range of mental health conditions, including depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, ADHD, trauma-related disorders, and other complex psychiatric challenges.

Associated with Akweli Mental Space in Lisle, Illinois, Catherine embraces an evidence-based, compassionate, and culturally responsive approach to mental healthcare. Her philosophy emphasizes understanding each individual's circumstances, identifying underlying contributors to mental health challenges, and developing personalized approaches that support long-term well-being.

As a Clinical Advisor to AlSakeena, Catherine brings valuable clinical perspectives to the organization's mission of restoring peace and economic dignity for individuals living with chronic mental health conditions. Her involvement will help inform AlSakeena's approach to mental health education, access to appropriate care, and the development of compassionate, person-centered support initiatives.
